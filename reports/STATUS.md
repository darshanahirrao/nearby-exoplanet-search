# Research status

Updated: 2026-09-07, Asia/Kolkata.

**Active research. No new or validated planet claimed.**

- Public catalogue retrieval and local TESS downloads are operational.
- Initial TOI-700 calibration recovered the known approximately 37.42-day planet,
  as well as aliases and signals failing held-back observations.
- A catalogue HZ label inconsistency was identified; boundaries are now calculated
  directly from the published equations.
- The initial 80-target batch and further calibration are running.
- Real-data injection/recovery and detailed independent vetting remain in progress.

Known-planet recoveries are calibration; synthetic injections are tests. Neither
constitutes a discovery. Verified results and limitations will be added here.
