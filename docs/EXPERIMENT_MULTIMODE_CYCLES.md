# Cycle exclusion with multiple variability modes

The first cycle-excluded experiment gained one strict injected recovery but
failed its gate. Two selected Wolf 1069 injections have detectable signals at
their known synthetic ephemerides yet remain missing when the ordinary search
retains eight fits. Merely increasing that fit budget did not recover them.
This motivates testing residual variability before a more expensive search redesign.

Permit at most three consecutive variability corrections, stopping when a pass
applies no model. Compare the production harmonic filter, the lower-threshold
harmonic filter, and the cycle-excluded median filter under the same three-pass
budget. The ordinary harmonic multi-pass method has already been used in a
targeted diagnostic; it is an essential comparison, not our invention.

Freeze 27 new period/phase/radius cases using seed 202609080 on the same three
development stars and radii 0.6, 0.8 and 1.0 Earth radii. Repeat the three unmodified
star diagnostics. All 90 runs use raw-light-curve injections, the unchanged
combined-season search, two retained peaks, and the original strict training and
reserved-sector recovery criteria. Do not tune this trial after reading its output.

Keep the preceding gate: at least two more strict recoveries than the stronger
harmonic comparison, no lost strict recovery from either comparison, and no
additional unflagged fits on unmodified stars relative to the lower-threshold
harmonic comparison. Passing only warrants new-star, realistic-transit validation.
These reused stars and box injections do not establish completeness, invention
priority or an astrophysical discovery.
